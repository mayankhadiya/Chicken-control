# Crowd Dominion v0.6

## Battle feel
- Added procedural projectile tracers from the cannon into the battle space.
- Added deterministic crowd-flow micro-animation.
- Added battle dust ambience.
- Added camera choreography that pulls toward the fortress during assault/critical phases.
- Added fortress damage-stage visual changes.
- Added combat score, combo counter, perfect-gate tracking, shot count and damage tracking.
- Added combo rewards and score-based victory coins.
- Added richer victory statistics.
- Added reward bonus for high combo runs.

## Rendering
- Retains Three.js InstancedMesh crowd rendering.
- Procedural geometry/materials only.
- Procedural Web Audio.
- Procedural particle effects.
