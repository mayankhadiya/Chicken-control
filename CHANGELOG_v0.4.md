# Crowd Dominion v0.4

## Core battle upgrade
- Explicit route state separate from cannon aim.
- Split and merge route sections.
- Route-sensitive crowd flow.
- Speed boosts that advance battle progress and increase army.
- Moving obstacles.
- Moving gates.
- Aggregate crowd combat with Champion power.
- Champion cooldown and shockwave.
- Staged fortress state: run, assault, critical, destroyed.
- Boss formations and boss health UI.
- More visible crowd representatives while keeping a logical army count.

## Meta upgrade
- 20 levels across 4 worlds.
- Missions for wins, gates, route sections, Champions and bosses.
- Persistent progression.
- Armory/base/shop retained.

## Rendering/audio
- Three.js WebGL only.
- Procedural geometry and CanvasTexture UI labels.
- Procedural Web Audio.
- Instanced crowd rendering.
- Procedural particle bursts.
